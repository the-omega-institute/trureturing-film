# 070 · AURIC–LANGLANDS II · CONTEXT AND SPECTRAL RECONSTRUCTION · AURIC–Langlands 第二部：语境与谱重构

This is the second film in the AURIC–Langlands series and runs about 6 minutes. It covers
`AURIC_LANGLANDS_CONTEXT_AND_SPECTRAL_RECONSTRUCTION_THEORY.md` in trureturing (§1–§12), at subject commit
`e9c9745321`.

Part one asked when new structure adds prediction. This volume asks three further questions:
- what a legal future is under permissions;
- what finite readings determine;
- how an explicit boundary model becomes a five-dimensional spectral local system, with every resource kept in view.

## The film, scene by scene

- **Legal futures (§2).**
  - For a finite permission automaton (Q, Σ, δ, q₀, F) acting on effects, the propagation
    U_q⁽ⁿ⁺¹⁾ = U_q⁽ⁿ⁾ + Σ A_a U_δ(q,a)⁽ⁿ⁾ spans exactly the accepted words of length at most n.
  - All spaces stabilise together within B = |Q|d − |F|r steps (Theorem 2.2).
  - A chain of length L shows nothing at q₀ for L steps (Example 2.3).
  - Any memory that reconstructs every declared future reply has at least as many states as there are future
    classes (Proposition 2.4). For the native task this means at least 11 states.
  - The spatial bit σ(P) = 1{m ≥ −17027/10} survives any reading error below 2.12659007 with two latch states
    (Corollary 2.5).
- **Context kernels (§3).**
  - The context blind kernel I_ctx is the largest tensor ideal inside the old blind space, so the quotient keeps its
    tensor structure (Theorem 3.2).
  - Real one-copy readings cannot separate I/2 + iaJ₁₂ from I/2, but real two-copy readings can (Example 3.3).
  - Strong symmetric monoidal functors on Rep(G) preserve dimension (Theorem 3.4).
  - Rep(G) for reductive G has only the trivial tensor ideals (Theorem 3.5).
  - In the category of completely positive trace-non-increasing maps, only one-dimensional objects are dualizable
    (Theorem 3.6).
- **Sources and references (§4–§5).**
  - Two independent sources give S, T and A⊗B (Theorem 4.2).
  - The commutator maps are equivariant surjections, both with image so₅: Λ²W₁₄ → so₅ with kernel 81 (Theorem 4.1)
    and Λ²so₅ → so₅ with kernel 35 (Theorem 4.2).
  - The commutator of readings is not a preparable phase reference (Proposition 4.3).
  - On a connected graph of joint observations only scales remain (Theorem 5.2): a global sign when some cycle is
    odd, and t against 1/t across a bipartite split. Positivity bounds |t|, and log-magnitudes are reconstructed with
    error at most ‖Δy‖/√λ_min(D + A) (Corollary 5.3).
  - Exact non-real unitaries from real processors are possible exactly when tr(σσ̄) = 0 (Theorem 5.4). The
    reference σ_θ = (I + θY)/2 never achieves this, for any number of copies (Corollary 5.5).
- **Spectral algebra (§6–§7).**
  - If δ_z ≠ 0 in a finite étale algebra, then 1, z, …, zⁿ⁻¹ is a basis and f = Σ c_j z^j with c = H⁻¹b. Here
    H_ij = Tr(z^{i+j}), b_i = Tr(f zⁱ) and det H = δ_z (Theorem 6.2).
  - Stability is a separate condition (Proposition 6.3). For z = (0, ε) the coefficient is 1/ε.
  - The project's exact collision W₂ ≠ W₃ with equal readings extends to a unique real-analytic family
    (Theorem 7.2). Along the family, B ≡ 0 or P_M ≡ 0 (Corollary 7.3).
- **Boundary responses (§8).**
  - The Schur response S(Q) = A − BK⁻¹Bᵀ is the energy minimum over the interior. Staged elimination equals
    one-shot elimination, d/dt S = Lᵀ Q̇ L, and ‖ΔS‖ ≤ (1 + M²/κ²)‖ΔQ‖ (Theorem 8.2).
  - Gluing along common ports is Lagrangian composition (Theorem 8.3).
  - On trees, d_ij = |det W_ij| obey the four-point law: of the three pairings, the minimum is attained at least
    twice. In logarithms this is the rank-two tropical Grassmann relation.
  - A measured violation certifies an error of at least (s₂ − s₁)/4 (Theorem 8.4).
- **Transfer to SO₅ (§9).**
  - T(D) = [[D, −I], [I, 0]] is symplectic. The blocks D = 0, E₁₁, E₂₂ and E₁₂ + E₂₁ generate a Zariski dense
    subgroup of Sp₄ (Theorem 9.2).
  - The primitive exterior square V₅ has signature (3, 2). It gives Sp₄(ℝ) → SO(3, 2) with kernel ±I, and
    Sp₄(ℂ) ↠ SO₅(ℂ) (Theorem 9.4).
- **Local system and lifts (§10).**
  - On a genus-four curve with a_j ↦ T(D_j) and b_j ↦ I, the composite ρ is Zariski dense in SO₅(ℂ) and admits no
    parabolic reduction (Theorem 10.4).
  - The Sp₄ lifts form a torsor under H¹(X, μ₂), which gives 2⁸ = 256 classes. No representation pulled back from
    SO₅ separates them (Theorem 10.5).
  - Under geometric Satake and unramified geometric Langlands (External Theorem 10.3), E has a nonzero Hecke
    eigen-object. The commutator maps κ and ℓ are Hecke natural (Theorem 10.6).
- **Green threshold (§11).**
  - For the fixed model, g_c = 1/a*, where a* is the best high-energy ratio of V to T, and 1/K ≤ g_c ≤ E_v/D_v.
  - Below g_c the form is closed and bounded below, with a ground state. Above g_c its infimum is −∞. At g_c it
    cannot be both bounded below and closed on the original domain (Theorem 11.2).
  - g_c(cA, dρ) = c²d · g_c(A, ρ) (Theorem 11.4).
