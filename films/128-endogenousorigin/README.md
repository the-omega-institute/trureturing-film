# 128 · AURIC ENDOGENOUS ORIGIN · MIRROR ORIGINS, RELATIONAL TUNNELLING AND HIDDEN PHASES · AURIC 内生原点 · 镜像原点、关系隧穿与隐藏相位

This is a standalone film and runs about 7¼ minutes. It covers one theory volume of trureturing at subject
commit `66d8447ec1`: `AURIC_ENDOGENOUS_ORIGIN_UNCERTAINTY_AND_RELATIONAL_TUNNELING.md`, §1–§6 and §8, with
Corollary 7.2 from §7.

An observer built from five modes cannot read its own origin from outside. The film follows what stays exact anyway:
which relations a record fixes, why two mirror origins share every distance, where the true energy barrier between
them lies, how sub-barrier states carry a current, why a shared position uncertainty leaves relative tunnelling
alone, how detuned bridges transfer or cancel, why an optimal recovery error cannot jump, and how a phase that no
current reading sees comes back under a later operation.

## The film, scene by scene

- **Records and fibres (§1, Proposition 1.3).**
  - A target R is identified by the record map π, R = R̄ ∘ π, exactly when R is constant on every nonempty fibre
    π⁻¹(y) (computation 1.3). A reading can fix a relation while the observer's full source stays open.
  - The even mixture of |L⟩ and |R⟩ and the state |+⟩ have the same branch probabilities (½, ½), but
    X = |L⟩⟨R| + |R⟩⟨L| reads 0 and 1 (computation 1.4).
  - With shared noise, X_i = A + r_i and X_0 = A + r_0 give X_i − X_0 = r_i − r_0 exactly, however wide A is
    (computation 1.6).
- **Two mirror origins (§2.1–§2.2, §2.4; Proposition 2.2, Theorem 2.3, Proposition 2.11).**
  - The five modes are placed in space as an origin o, three fixed endpoints p₁, p₂, p₃ and a diagonal point
    d = p₁ + p₃ − o. The closure law is a chosen interpretation.
  - With αᵢ = (s_ij + s_ik − s_jk)/2, nonzero orthogonal legs from o exist exactly when α₁, α₂, α₃ > 0, that is,
    when the endpoint triangle is acute. Then there are exactly two origins o± = H ± h n, where H is the orthocentre,
    h² = (Σ αᵢ⁻¹)⁻¹ and ‖pᵢ − o±‖² = αᵢ. A right triangle leaves one origin (the right-angle vertex) and an obtuse
    triangle none.
  - Reflection in the endpoint plane swaps o₊ with o₋ and d₊ with d₋, so the two five-point completions share all ten
    pairwise distances (2.5). On screen: α = (1, 2, 3), h² = 6/11, squared distances 1, 2, 3, 4, 3, 4, 3, 5, 6, 1 for
    both.
  - The chirality sign det(p₁ − o, p₃ − o, p₂ − o) is kept on an origin error ball of radius ε_o exactly when
    ε_o < dist(o, Π), which is h at o±.
- **The true barrier (§2.6, Propositions 2.14 and 2.15).**
  - The penalty V_full = (k/2) Σ g_ij² restricted to the normal line is V(q) = Λ(q² − h²)² with Λ = 3k/2: a quartic
    double well with barrier Λh⁴, V″(±h) = 8Λh² and V″(0) = −4Λh².
  - For the endpoints e₁, e₂, e₃ the normal midpoint costs k/6, but the min-max height over all continuous paths
    between the two origins is exactly k/8. A path that swerves sideways through r² = 1/6 attains it.
- **Current under the barrier (§3, Theorems 3.5 and 3.6, Corollary 3.7, Proposition 3.8).**
  - With an effective mass m, if 2π²ħ²/(mΛh⁶) < 175/256 then 0 < E_e < E_o < V_b (Theorem 3.5 with δ = h/4).
  - Starting in |L⟩ = (φ_e − φ_o)/√2, the right-side probability is P₊(t) = ½ − c cos(ΔE t/ħ) with
    0 < c < ½, and the current at the forbidden centre is j(0, t) = (ΔE c/ħ) sin(ΔE t/ħ) (Theorem 3.6).
  - On screen: the residual bound ⟨(q² − h²)²⟩ ≤ E/Λ, and the operator identity ô + d̂ = (p₁ + p₃)I with
    Cov(n·ô, n·d̂) = −Var q.
- **The observer as a quantum body (§4, Proposition 4.2).**
  - For an observer of mass M and a target of mass m with a potential of their separation only,
    H = P²/2(M + m) + p²/2μ + V(r) with μ = mM/(m + M). The relative reduced state evolves by H_rel alone for every
    initial state, product or correlated. With the relative state fixed, a centre-of-mass uncertainty leaves relative
    tunnelling unchanged.
  - Under a rectangular barrier, T(E) = [1 + V₀² sinh²(κa)/(4E(V₀ − E))]⁻¹ > 0 for 0 < E < V₀ (4.5).
- **Detuned bridges (§5.1–§5.4, Theorems 5.5 and 5.9, Corollary 5.6).**
  - In an added Hamiltonian on the five modes, the null mode L and the joint mode R are joined through two bridges
    (modes 1 and 3) at detuning Δ, with mode 2 as a leaf. Then PU_tP = f_t(M) with M = BB†, the five eigenvalues are
    (Δ ± Ω_μ±)/2 and Δ, and the interior weight is at most 4μ₊/(Δ² + 4μ₊) for all t.
  - With no leaf and zero phase, Δₙ = 4Jn/√(2n + 1) and Tₙ = πħ√(2n + 1)/(2J) give U(Tₙ)|L⟩ = −|R⟩, while the
    interior weight never exceeds (2n + 1)/(2(n + 1)²) → 0 (Theorem 5.9).
- **Dark phase (§5.5, Theorems 5.10 and 5.11, Corollaries 5.12 and 7.2).**
  - At loop phase φ = π the transfer amplitude ⟨R|U_t|L⟩ is zero for all t and every leaf coupling g ≥ 0. At φ = 0,
    with the same vertices and edge moduli, it is −J²t²/ħ² + O(t³) (Corollary 7.2).
  - With g = 0 and Δ > 2J, every finite window has P_T(φ) ≤ T²J²|φ − π|²/ħ², while over all time
    P_∞(φ) ≥ (1 − 4J²/Δ²)² for φ ≠ π, and P_∞(π) = 0. The waiting time grows like πħ√(Δ² + 8J²)/(2J²|φ − π|).
  - A fixed leaf g > 0 gives P_∞ ≤ 4J⁴|φ − π|²/g⁴, so the jump at infinite time disappears (Corollary 5.12).
- **Fixed recovery task (§6, Proposition 6.2).**
  - For the qutrit channel F = U₀Δ_W applied n times and the target E_p = |v_p⟩⟨v_p| with v_p = √p ξ + √(1 − p) e,
    the optimal worst-case error over same-space decoders equals η_n(E_p) = min over effects B of
    ‖(Fⁿ)†(B) − E_p‖_op, and D_B(X) = Tr(BX)E_p + Tr((I − B)X)(I − E_p)/2 attains it.
  - |η_n(E_p) − η_n(E_p′)| ≤ |√(p(1 − p′)) − √(p′(1 − p))| (6.7), and on [δ, 1 − δ] the bound is
    |p − p′|/(2√(δ(1 − δ))). A change of root, certificate family or optimal effect therefore cannot make the optimal
    error jump.
- **Hidden phase, returned (§8.3–§8.5, Propositions 8.8, 8.10 and 8.16, Example 8.11).**
  - From the uniform superposition of the five modes, N joint phase gates e^(−iθQ) keep every probability at 1/5 and
    the means at (2/5, 2/5, 1/5), but set ζ = ψ₀ψ₁₃/(ψ₁ψ₃) = e^(−iNθ). For a diagonal Hamiltonian,
    dζ/dt = −(iJ/ħ)ζ with J = E₁₃ − E₁ − E₃ + E₀.
  - The endpoint pulse R₁(α) gives X = 2/5 + (1/5) sin 2α sin φ, Y = 2/5, Z = 1/5. With θ = π/2 and α = π/4 the pulse
    reads (2/5, 2/5, 1/5) after no gate and (3/5, 2/5, 1/5) after one; the second output has probabilities
    (1/5, 1/5, 1/5, 0, 2/5).
- **What averages predict (§8.6–§8.11; Application 8.20, Proposition 8.22, Example 8.23, Propositions 8.28 and 8.31,
  Example 8.32).**
  - The current means predict the next means for every density matrix exactly when U†WU ⊆ W, with
    W = span{I, N_x, N_y, N_z}. For the endpoint pulse the residual is D_α = (sin 2α/2)K with ‖D_α‖²_HS = sin² 2α, so
    W is kept only when sin 2α = 0. Otherwise ρ± = I/5 ± D_α/5 agree now and their later N_x readings are
    2/5 ± sin² 2α/5.
  - A Bell pair |Φ_φ⟩ has both local states I/2 while ⟨σx⊗σx⟩ = cos φ and ⟨σx⊗σy⟩ = sin φ.
  - Two four-band spectra (1, 1, 1, 1) and (1 − η, 1 − η, 1 − η, 1 + η) both read (2, 2, 2). A filter that passes
    only the fourth band returns (1, 1, 1) and (1 + η)(1, 1, 1).
