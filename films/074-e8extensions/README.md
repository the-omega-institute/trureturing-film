# 074 · AURIC–E8 · COMPATIBLE EXTENSIONS AND OBSERVATION · AURIC–E8 · 相容扩张与观察理论

This is a standalone film and runs about 7 minutes. It takes the AURIC observation framework to E8 and covers the whole of
`AURIC_E8_COMPATIBLE_EXTENSIONS_AND_OBSERVATION_THEORY.md` at trureturing subject commit `f15ddb4c42`: the results of
§1–§9, theorem numbers 2.1–9.1. §10 lists formalization targets and has no further results.

Two five-mode interfaces each carry an A₄ charge lattice. The film asks four questions in turn. Which integral extensions
of A₄ ⊕ A₄ exist? Which central phases can an actual observer see? When do local projective data lift globally? What
can a golden readout a + φb recover at finite precision?

## The film, scene by scene

- **Integral glue (§2.1, Lemma 2.1, Theorem 2.3).**
  - Q = A₄ has det 5 and 20 roots. Its discriminant group is Q*/Q ≅ F₅, generated by ω₁, with pairing 4aa′/5 mod ℤ.
  - Class k has minimal norm k(5 − k)/5, attained by C(5, k) vectors.
  - An extension L of L₀ = Q ⊕ Q with integer pairings satisfies L₀ ⊂ L ⊂ L₀*. So C = L/L₀ is a totally isotropic
    subspace of F₅².
  - A nonzero (a, b) in C needs a² + b² ≡ 0, so (b/a)² = −1 = 4 and b = ±2a. Exactly two lines survive, C₊ and C₋. They
    give Γ₊ = L₀ + ℤ(ω₁, ω₂) and Γ₋ = L₀ + ℤ(ω₁, ω₃), each of index 5.
  - The map (x, y) ↦ (x, −y) makes Γ₊ and Γ₋ isometric. It does not preserve the local markings.
- **Automatic E8 (Theorem 2.3, Corollary 2.4, Proposition 2.5).**
  - For x ∈ Γ±, 5x lies in the even lattice L₀, and x² ∈ ℤ. Since 25 is odd, x² is even.
  - det Γ± = 25/5² = 1.
  - The roots are 40 + 4 · 50 = 240. The irreducible rank-8 simply laced systems A₈, D₈ and E₈ have 72, 112 and 240 roots,
    so Γ± is the E8 root lattice. The step uses the classical ADE classification.
  - One new vector x ∉ L₀ with integral pairings and integral norm already gives E8.
  - For the recipe (ω₁, ω_k) of norm 2 on two A_(m−1), (k − 1)m = k² + 1 forces (m, k) = (5, 2) or (5, 3).
- **Five-grading and central phase (Proposition 3.1, Theorem 3.2, Proposition 3.4).**
  - With Γ₊, the glue class grades E8 as g₀ ⊕ … ⊕ g₄, with [g_j, g_k] ⊂ g_(j+k).
  - The pieces are g₀ = sl(V) ⊕ sl(W), g₁ = V ⊗ Λ²W, g₂ = Λ²V ⊗ W*, g₃ = (Λ²V)* ⊗ W and g₄ = V* ⊗ (Λ²W)*. Their
    dimensions are 48, 50, 50, 50 and 50.
  - (ζᵃI, ζᵇI) acts on g_j by ζ^(j(a+2b)). It is trivial exactly when b = 2a, the glue slope.
  - So H = (SU(5) × SU(5))/C with C = {(ζᵃI, ζ^(2a)I)}, and 1 → μ₅ → H → PU(5)² → 1. Each local projective pair has
    exactly five lifts.
  - Every continuous map E8 → U(5) is trivial, since 248 > 25.
- **Phase observation (Theorem 4.1, Corollary 4.2, Theorem 4.3, Corollary 4.4).**
  - Phases a and b are equivalent for the allowed effects exactly when (a − b)d = 0 for every grade difference d that
    occurs in a block P_j A P_k ≠ 0.
  - Block-diagonal effects see none of the five phases. One cross-grade block, with all input states allowed, separates
    all five, because 5 is prime.
  - One fixed effect can still confuse a and −a. For ψ = (e₀ + e₁)/√2 and E = |ψ⟩⟨ψ|, P(a) = (1 + cos 2πa/5)/2.
  - Take a probe ψ = Σ √w_j e_j, with the probes preparable and five-outcome POVMs allowed. The optimal one-shot
    success is (Σ √w_j)²/5, attained by the Fourier POVM.
  - This equals 1 only for equal weights, and r nonzero weights give at most r/5. The equal-weight mixture over the
    grades gives only 1/5.
- **Lift obstruction (Theorems 5.1–5.2, Example 5.3).**
  - Let ρ = (ρ₁, ρ₂) : Π → PU(5)². The obstruction to lifting into H is o_H = o₁ + 2o₂ in H²(Π; F₅). A lift exists
    exactly when o₂ = 2o₁, and the lifts then form an H¹(Π; F₅)-torsor.
  - On the torus, the clock and shift matrices D and S satisfy DS = ζSD and D²S = ζ²SD².
  - Neither ([D], [S]) nor ([D²], [S]) lifts to SU(5). Yet ([(D, D²)], [(S, S)]) lifts to H, because (ζI, ζ²I) ∈ C.
  - With a trivial second factor the joint class is 1 and no lift exists.
- **Face defects (Theorems 5.4 and 5.6).**
  - On a finite 2-complex with flat edge labels, any edge lifts give face defects η ∈ C²(B; F₅). Shifting by central
    edge phases b gives η′ = η + δb.
  - A lift closing every face exists exactly when [η] = 0 in H²(B; F₅). The closing lifts form an H¹(B; F₅)-torsor,
    with 5^(b₁) classes on a connected graph.
  - Supply the uniform grade probe and the Fourier measurement. One turn around a face then reads η_f with probability 1.
- **Control and cost (Theorem 6.1, Proposition 2.6, Theorems 6.3, 6.5 and 6.6).**
  - su(5) ⊕ su(5) plus any X in the compact e₈ outside it, with all e^(tX), generates the whole compact e₈ (dimension
    248). Brackets walk the grades 1 → 2 → 4 → 3 → 1.
  - On the plain product ℂ⁵ ⊗ ℂ⁵ the same recipe gives su(25), of dimension 624.
  - A fixed generating menu has a uniform finite word length N(S). Its value is not computed.
  - Almost every target needs at least 248 tunable pulses.
  - With all local gates free, almost every target needs at least 5 relation pulses, because 49r + 48 = 244 < 248 at
    r = 4. Five is not shown to be sufficient or optimal.
- **Golden readout (Theorems 7.2–7.5).**
  - P₊(a, b) = a + φb is injective on integer pairs. Its inverse is discontinuous everywhere: vₙ = (F_(n+1), −Fₙ) grows
    without bound while P₊vₙ = σⁿ → 0.
  - At height H, 1/(2φH) ≤ δ_H < φ/(2H). A decoder for closed noise ε exists exactly when 2ε < δ_H.
  - The joint readout (a + φb, a + σb) has minimal sup-gap exactly 1. The whole lattice ℤ^(2d) decodes exactly when
    ε < 1/2.
- **Windows (Theorems 8.1, 8.4 and 8.6, Proposition 8.7).**
  - Take a bounded window W with nonempty interior and diameter D on the hidden conjugate. Distinct readouts then differ
    by at least 1/D, and the inverse is Lipschitz with constant C_D.
  - The readouts form an aperiodic Delone set with finite local complexity.
  - T(a, b) = (b, a + b) gives P₊T = φP₊ and P₋T = σP₋, so Λ(σⁿW) = φⁿΛ(W). For star-shaped W, φ²Λ(W) is a proper
    subset of Λ(W).
  - N(a, b) = a² + ab − b² has signature (d, d), N(Tv) = −N(v) and N(T²v) = N(v).
- **Sign selection (Theorem 9.1).**
  - With both markings restored, H_s = SU(5)²/C_s has obstruction o₁ + 2s·o₂. A flat H_s lift exists for some sign
    exactly when o₂ = ±2o₁.
  - If o₁ = o₂ = 0, both signs work. Otherwise, at most one sign works.
  - For the torus, H² = F₅. Of the 25 pairs, 1 allows both signs, 4 allow only s = +, 4 allow only s = − and 16 allow
    neither. The clock and shift example, (o₁, o₂) = (1, 2), selects s = +.
